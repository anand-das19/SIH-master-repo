# SMART IRRIGATION SYSTEM — PREDICTIVE MODEL & DECISION ENGINE
## Architecture & Execution Strategy (Binary Classification MVP)

### 1. Executive Summary & Objective
* **Goal**: Develop a high-accuracy, production-grade predictive irrigation model and automated decision engine using our primary dataset (dataset/data.csv — 16,411 rows, clean, zero missing values).
* **Scope**: Laser-focused prototype spanning Phases 1 to 4 (Data Ingestion/EDA, Preprocessing & Stratified Splits, Dual ML+DL Model Benchmarking, and Decision & CLI Demonstration Suite).
* **Target Outcome**: A robust, edge-ready prototype with verifiable benchmarks (F1 > 90%, ROC-AUC > 0.95), inference latency < 2ms, and an interactive CLI demonstration script (demo_predict.py) for live judging and frontend integration.

---

### 2. Feature & Target Formulation
* **Input Features (3 Core IoT Sensors)**:
  1. MOI: Soil Moisture Percentage (Range: 1% to 100%)
  2. temp: Ambient Temperature in Celsius (Range: 13 to 46)
  3. humidity: Relative Humidity Percentage (Range: 15.0 to 91.0)
* **Target Formulation (Binary Classification)**:
  - need_irrigation:
    - 0: No Irrigation (Pump OFF — 0 mins) [55.22% of dataset]
    - 1: Irrigation Required (Pump ON — dynamically calculated 15 to 40 mins) [44.78% of dataset]
* **Dual-Model Strategy (ML + DL Head-to-Head)**:
  1. Model 1: Gradient Boosted Trees (XGBoost / LightGBM) — Ultra-fast, lightweight, near-zero inference latency, production edge ready.
  2. Model 2: Deep Neural Network (ANN / MLP) — Multi-layer perceptron with Dropout and BatchNorm capturing non-linear feature interactions as a deep learning benchmark for judges.
